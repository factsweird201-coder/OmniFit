READY TO TRAIN?
Put your device on the floor about 2 m away so your shoulders, elbows and hands are in view. Your training video never leaves your device.

How it counts

  • Works facing the camera or side-on. Hold the top position for a second so it learns your height.
  • Go down until Depth hits ~40% and push back up. Hit 50%+ for a deep rep.
  • Every push-up counts toward your lifetime total and your rank — in training and in boss fights.

How boss fights work

      
    
        
  • Every push-up hits the boss. Deep reps (50%+) are critical hits for 1.5× damage.
    • 
        
  • Combo: keep reps within 3 seconds of each other to stack bonus damage.
    • 
        
  • Attacks: when the boss winds up (red bar), do a push-up before it lands to PARRY for double damage. Miss it and you lose a heart. Lose all 3 and you're defeated.
    • 
        
  • Specials: 🛡️ Armor — only deep reps hurt. ⚡ Weak point — double damage, go fast! 💚 Regenerating — hit it 3 times to stop it. 🌀 Rapid — attacks come twice as fast.
    • 
        
  • Beat a boss to unlock the next. Finish with all 3 hearts for ⭐⭐⭐.
    • 
      

    

    

      
How 1v1 battles work

      
    
        
  • Create a battle and send the 5-letter code (or invite link) to a friend. They open 1v1, type the code and hit Join.
    • 
        
  • You see each other live. Video goes straight between your two devices — never uploaded or recorded. Untick Show my camera to share only a stick figure.
    • 
        
  • Every push-up hits your opponent for 10. Deep reps (50%+) crit for 1.5×. Reps within 3 s of each other build a combo bonus.
    • 
        
  • Knockout: first to drain 200 HP wins (3 min cap). 60s Blitz: most damage in a minute wins.
    • 
        
  • Same push-up checks as training — sitting down or waving your arms doesn't score.
