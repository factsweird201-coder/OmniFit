Today's workout

Your trainer rotates push days, squat days and sit-up days. It watches every rep (how many, how deep, how fast, and where you slowed down) and moves your next target up, holds it, or eases off.

How it counts

  • Stand the camera up 2–3 m away, around hip height, so your whole body is in view — facing it or side-on.
  • Stand tall for a second so it learns your height, then sit back and down. Depth hits 50% when your thighs are about parallel to the floor. 40%+ counts as a deep rep.
  • Squats get their own lifetime total, best set and daily goal. They also power the 🦵 Squat Campaign. Your rank still comes from push-ups.
  • Put the camera on the floor 2–3 m to your side, so it sees you side-on: shoulders and hips in view.
  • Lie flat on your back, knees bent, then sit all the way up. A rep counts when you sit up past halfway from lying down. Sit up tall (about 60°+) for a full rep.
  • Sit-ups get their own lifetime total, best set and daily goal. They also power the 🛏️ Sit-Up Campaign. Your rank still comes from push-ups.
  • Works facing the camera or side-on. Hold the top position for a second so it learns your height.
  • Go down until Depth hits ~30% and push back up. Hit 40%+ for a deep rep.
  • Every push-up counts toward your lifetime total and your rank — in training and in boss fights.

How the campaign works

      
    
        
  • The story: the Great Ember went out and the grey Slump is creeping over the land. Every push-up throws off a spark of Ember, so in every mission your reps power something: light the brazier, drop the gate, push the cart, build the bridge.
    • 
        
  • Two parts, 8 chapters, 48 missions. Part 1, The Great Ember, takes you from Boot Camp to the Void Eye. Beat it to unlock Part 2, The Hollow King: the coast, the Gearworks, Skyreach, the Dreamless Kingdom, and a rival named Vex. Switch parts with the tabs on the map.
    • 
        
  • Finish a mission to unlock the next one; each chapter has two bosses. Story scenes play the first time; tap 📜 Story on the map to watch one again.
    • 
        
  • Mission types: 💪 reach a count · ⏱️ beat the clock · 🎯 deep reps only (40%+) · 🔗 no resting over 4 s · 🔥 as many as you can before time runs out · ⚔️ boss fights.
    • 
        
  • Earn up to ⭐⭐⭐ per mission — replay any mission to improve your stars.
    • 
        
  • Push-up counts are tuned to the level you picked in your profile. Every push-up still counts toward your rank.
    • 
        
  • 🦵 Squat Campaign: a whole separate campaign done with squats, in two parts: The Sinking Lands and The Heavy Sky (unlocks after Queen Quagmire). 8 chapters, 48 missions and 16 bosses. Switch campaigns with the buttons at the top of the map.
    • 
        
  • 🛏️ Sit-Up Campaign: The Long Nap. Count Slumber has put the whole world to sleep, and in Part Two, The Comfy Kingdom, Queen Comfy wants everyone stuck on the sofa: 8 chapters, 48 missions and 16 bosses, all done with sit-ups. Lie on your back side-on to the camera so it can see your shoulders and hips. Sit all the way up for crits.
    • 
      

    

    

      
How Endurance races work

      
    
        
  • An Endurance race is 8 runs, each one followed by a workout station, and it needs no equipment: each run is a different cardio move the camera counts (high knees, jumping jacks, butt kicks, skaters, squat jumps) and every station is a bodyweight swap.
    • 
        
  • Stand the camera up 2–3 m away, around hip height, so your whole body is in view, standing and on the floor.
    • 
        
  • Counted parts end when you hit the target. Wall sit, plank and mountain climbers are timed. The clock never stops, so moving fast between parts is part of the race.
    • 
        
  • Camera missed something? Press Skip. The race still finishes, but it won't count as a PB.
    • 
        
  • Push-ups and squats you do here count toward your lifetime totals. Choose Lite, Open or Pro, and each one keeps its own PB.
    • 
      

    

    

      
How boss fights work

      
    
        
  • Every push-up hits the boss. Deep reps (40%+) are critical hits for 1.5× damage.
    • 
        
  • Combo: keep reps within 3 seconds of each other to stack bonus damage.
    • 
        
  • Attacks: when the boss winds up (red bar), do a push-up before it lands to PARRY for double damage. Miss it and you lose a heart. Lose all 3 and you're defeated.
    • 
        
  • Specials: 🛡️ Armor — only deep reps hurt. ⚡ Weak point — double damage, go fast! 💚 Regenerating — hit it 3 times to stop it. 🌀 Rapid — attacks come twice as fast.
    • 
        
  • Beat a boss to unlock the next. Finish with all 3 hearts for ⭐⭐⭐.
